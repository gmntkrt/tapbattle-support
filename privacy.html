Tap Battle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Tap Battle is a local multiplayer mobile game developed by Osman Kurt. We respect your privacy and aim to provide a simple and safe gaming experience.

1. Information Collection

Tap Battle does not require registration or user accounts. The app does not intentionally collect personal information or transmit gameplay information to the developer's servers.

2. Advertising and Analytics

Tap Battle does not contain advertisements or third-party analytics SDKs.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information collected through the app.

4. Children's Privacy

Tap Battle is designed for a general audience, including families. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Third-Party Services

Tap Battle does not require third-party accounts or external services for gameplay. Apple may independently process information relating to App Store downloads and device operation under Apple's own privacy policies.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
